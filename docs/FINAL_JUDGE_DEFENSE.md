# Final Judge Defense

1. **Why not just SuccessFactors?** SAP provides workforce context; RE:WORK reasons across evidence, gaps, proxies, pathways, proof, and two-sided viability.

2. **Why not Opportunity Marketplace?** RE:WORK does not rank opportunities — it diagnoses viability, interventions, and employer readiness around them.

3. **Why not Learning recommendations?** RE:WORK binds learning to diagnosed gaps and requires proof-of-skill before capability upgrade.

4. **What is agentic?** Candidate Intelligence and Job Decomposition agents discover and decompose; LangGraph orchestrates.

5. **What is deterministic?** Diagnosis, pathway, proof, viability, intervention simulation — governed engines, not LLM prose.

6. **What does SAP provide?** Workforce, skills, learning catalog, opportunity context (SIMULATED in demo).

7. **What does RE:WORK add?** Evidence synthesis, diagnosis, counterfactuals, minimum-effective intervention, proof, explainability, human governance.

8. **SAP unavailable?** SIMULATED mode with explicit labeling; demo continues.

9. **LLM fails?** DEMO_FALLBACK; engine mode displayed.

10. **Explainability?** Structured reports + evidence chains — not chatbot prose.

11. **Discriminatory ranking?** No protected attributes in ranking; governance invariants in benchmark.

12. **Skill validation?** Proof-of-skill engine + reassessment; SAP completion not fabricated.

13. **Counterfactual importance?** Separates genuine gaps from eligibility proxies requiring human review.

14. **Employer readiness?** Two-sided viability — candidate gap + employer gap.

15. **Intervention simulation?** Projects minimum-effective changes — labeled SIMULATED PROJECTION.

16. **Smallest useful product?** One case: diagnose gap → pathway → proof → viability → human decision.

**One-liner:** RE:WORK turns "not a match" into "what is actually missing, what is merely a barrier, and what is the smallest intervention that can make the opportunity viable?"
